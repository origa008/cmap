The maths of a public MBBS seat

Pillar 4 · Exam plan

MDCAT, dated

  • 2026 sitting: Sunday 20 Sept 2026 (rescheduled from 16 Aug); 138,158 candidates registered.↗
  • Format: 180 MCQs — Biology 81, Chemistry 45, Physics 36, English 9, Logical Reasoning 9. No negative marking.

Pillar 4 · Exam plan

ECAT, dated

  • 2026 phases: Phase I 30 Mar–3 Apr; Phase II 16/17–21 June. Two attempts a year; the best score counts.↗
  • Format: 100 MCQs in 100 minutes, 400 marks — English 10, Physics 30, Maths 30, Chemistry / CS / Stats 30. No negative marking; computer-based.
  • Fee: Rs 3,000 per attempt (2026). Minimum 60% in Inter for engineering; aggregate at least 50%.
  • ECAT 2027: not announced yet — expect a similar Mar–Jun pattern.
  • Prep: FSc Part-I is half your aggregate — protect it. ECAT practice from February.
Pillar 5 · Job path

Engineer — the degree alone doesn't place you

  • Skills that pay: AutoCAD / SolidWorks (Mech, Civil), PLC & embedded systems (Electrical), Python + data tools (all trades).
  • Government route: PPSC / FPSC engineering posts and WAPDA / NTDC — your roadmap lists current openings and syllabi.
Engineering graduate unemployment is 23.5% (PIDE) — doubled in two years. The students who get hired are the ones with skills on top of the degree.↗

Pillar 1 · Where you stand

The test decides your seat, not your marks

BBA admissions at the top schools are test-based. Your 82% clears every eligibility bar — the entry test decides where you land. IBA BBA is ≈Rs 260,000/semester; LUMS BBA is in the Rs 4.4–5.9 lakh range (2026 listings vary) — so IBA and NUST are your realistic targets, LUMS only with NOP aid.

IBA Karachi is a public autonomous institution (Government of Sindh) — not a private university — which is why its fee sits well below LUMS.
Pillar 2 · Field decision

Six realistic paths

  • BBA at IBA Karachi: ≈Rs 260,000/semester; test-based admission.
  • BBA at NUST: Rs 275,400/semester (Business / Social Sciences); NET-based.↗
  • BBA at LUMS: ≈Rs 4.4–5.9 lakh/semester — apply for NOP (full tuition for low-income students) if eligible.
  • CA (ICAP): start after Inter; trainee stipend Rs 25,000–60,000/month during articles; a qualified CA earns Rs 150,000–300,000+/month.
  • ACCA: global qualification; exemptions for prior study via the ACCA exemptions calculator (accaglobal.com).
  • BCom / BS Accounting (public university): the lowest fees — check pu.edu.pk and uok.edu.pk for current fee schedules.
